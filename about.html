About Me

Design • Technology • Creativity

My name is Shantell Vine, and my work brings together web and digital design, cybersecurity technology, and traditional art.

I am pursuing degrees in Web and Digital Design and Cybersecurity Technology, along with a minor in Art at the University of Maryland Global Campus.

Through my academic projects, I have developed experience with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, website development, cloud computing, and cybersecurity concepts. My artwork includes traditional drawing and painting.

I am especially interested in applying my creative and technical skills to independently design and maintain websites for my own business ventures.

This portfolio highlights selected academic projects, creative work, and technical experiences that reflect my continued development across these disciplines.
